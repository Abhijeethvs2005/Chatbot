## 🌟 Features

### 1. 🦾 3D Procedural Robot System (WebGL + Three.js)
- **Articulated Mecha Architecture**: Procedural 3D robot chassis with carbon-fiber frame, rotating dual gyroscope rings around an illuminated quantum core reactor, and dual ion plasma thruster exhausts with dynamic particle physics.
- **Dynamic 2D Canvas Visor Display**: An interactive digital face screen rendered directly onto the 3D curved visor:
  - `idle_blink` (natural breathing and blink cycle)
  - `happy_smile` (cheerful crescent eyes)
  - `thinking_matrix` (revolving neural radar scan rings)
  - `speaking_waves` (audio spectrum visualizer synchronized with speech synthesis)
  - `surprised` (curious wide eyes)
- **Inverse Kinematics Head Tracking**: Smoothly tracks mouse cursor or touch points across the screen.
- **Multi-Palette Theme Switcher**:
  - 🦾 **Cyber Cyan** (Obsidian Carbon & Electric Cyan)
  - ⚠️ **Amber Hazard** (Industrial Hazard Amber & Slate)
  - 🌌 **Quantum Void** (Deep Void & Neon Hyper-Violet)
  - 🧪 **Cleanroom White** (MIT Robotics Cleanroom Slate & Laser Blue)
- **Camera Presets & Overdrive**:
  - `🎥 Command View`
  - `🔍 Core Diagnostic View`
  - `⚡ Turbo Overdrive` (Spins gyroscope rings, accelerates thruster flare, and pulses reactor!)

---

### 2. ⚡ Fastest Answering Power (< 10ms Latency) & High Accuracy
- **Sub-10ms Academic Knowledge Core**: Built-in curriculum knowledge graph spanning **Calculus, Classical Mechanics, Electromagnetism, QuickSort & Big-O, Neural Networks, DNA Replication, Gibbs Free Energy, and Study Hacks**.
- **Pedagogical Structure**:
  1. 📌 **Core Definition**: Precise definition of concepts.
  2. 📐 **KaTeX Mathematical Proofs**: Crisp LaTeX formulation ($$E=mc^2$$, Chain Rule, Maxwell's Equations, Time Complexities).
  3. 🔬 **Step-by-Step Breakdown**: Logical derivation from first principles.
  4. 💡 **Intuitive Analogy (ELI5)**: Concrete mental models.
  5. ⚠️ **High-Frequency Exam Traps**: Critical warnings on common test mistakes.
  6. 🎯 **Active Recall Quiz Generator**: Instant multiple-choice tests with XP rewards.
  7. 📈 **Interactive 2D Function Grapher**: Real-time canvas plotter for calculus and physics functions.

### 3. 🎒 Student Workflow & Robotics Dashboard
- **⏱️ Integrated Pomodoro Focus Clock**: 25-minute study sessions with audio feedback.

## 🎮 Interacting with Chatbot Abhi

| Action | How to Trigger |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Track Cursor** | Move your mouse across the screen — Abhi's head follows in real-time. |
| **High Five / Poke** | Click directly on the 3D robot or click the **"👋 HIGH FIVE"** button. |
| **Spin Trick** | Click **"🔄 SPIN"** on the bottom HUD. |
| **Turbo Overdrive** | Click **"⚡ OVERDRIVE"** to activate high-speed thinking mode. |
| **Change Theme** | Click any theme circle in the top bar (Cyan, Amber, Violet, White). |
| **Voice Question** | Click the **🎤** microphone button and speak your query. |
| **Take a Quiz** | Click **"🎯 Test My Knowledge"** on any explanation. |
| **Save to Notes** | Click **"🎒 Save to Notes"** and review anytime in the notes drawer. |
